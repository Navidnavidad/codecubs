About CodeCubs Academy

Founded by Mohamed Naveed Mohamed Rafi

CodeCubs Academy was founded by Mohamed Naveed Mohamed Rafi with a simple belief: every child can learn to code when lessons are patient, playful and built around things they love. What began as helping family and friends with their first programs has grown into an online academy that pairs live tutoring with books kids can keep learning from long after a lesson ends.

Our tutoring covers Scratch for younger learners, then Python, web design and game development as students grow. Our books are written to match those same steps, so children can practise between lessons or start on their own. We keep classes small, explain ideas without jargon, and celebrate every bug fixed along the way.

Our mission is to give children the confidence to create with technology, not just use it.
